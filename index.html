Install on Android

Open this site in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen. The generated website recipe text is included with the app. Use “Save all bundled food photos offline” before relying on the gallery without internet access.

Source and accuracy

Cookbook-exclusive recipes are transcribed from your photographs and include the scans for verification. Website recipes are generated from the publisher's public recipe pages by the repository workflow and bundled into the app. OCR-draft entries are marked so they can be checked against the scan.
